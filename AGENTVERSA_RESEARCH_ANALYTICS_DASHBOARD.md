# AgentVersa Research Analytics Dashboard

The dashboard is available from the `Open research dashboard` button in `legalverse-k17-analysis`. It displays stored-data counts, eight-dimension evaluation summaries, prediction/observation comparison counts, evidence/claim traceability counts, scenario status, progress tracking, and a JSON export.

Data is stored in browser local storage under `agentversa-research-platform-v1`. The export includes a generation timestamp and methodology note. No missing score, observation, experiment, or evidence record is converted into a fabricated result.

The dashboard is a single-researcher prototype. It does not provide authentication, multi-user conflict resolution, a server-side audit log, statistical testing, or an automated claim verifier.
