# Evidence Traceability System

The research store has separate collections for evidence, claims, many-to-many claim/evidence mappings, and audit entries. Evidence verification and claim verification are independent. Relationship types include Supports, Contradicts, Provides Context, Partially Supports, Does Not Establish, and Not Relevant.

The dashboard reports unmapped claims and contradictory mappings without resolving them automatically. Evidence quality should be reviewed using documented criteria for authenticity, reliability, relevance, completeness, corroboration, timeliness, and limitations. A source may be authentic without establishing every claim derived from it.

The current repository supplies narrative source documents, not a complete structured registry. Therefore the initial evidence and claim collections are empty and require researcher entry.
